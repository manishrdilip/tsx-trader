## Owner-approved objective: maximum net profit (2026-11-26)

This section governs candidate selection and supersedes any earlier weighted score or win-rate target. The first official campaign remains exactly 1 billion unique eligible evaluations; a trillion rapid signal screens is a separate stretch target and must never be counted as full backtests.

### Objective and universe
- Maximize compounded, executable net profit after all costs on fixed starting capital of CAD 5,000 or USD 5,000, in separate account tracks. On identical dates and capital, maximizing terminal equity is equivalent to maximizing net profit; report CAGR too. Do not rank raw dollar profits across currencies or unequal periods as comparable.
- Win rate is diagnostic only: no minimum win-rate gate, no win-rate weight, and no optimization of win rate at the expense of profit. Report average winner/loser, payoff ratio, expectancy and trade count.
- Preserve profit-first leaderboards and a Pareto frontier of net profit, mark-to-market drawdown, tail loss and stability. Show risk bands at <=10%, <=20%, <=30%, <=40%, and unrestricted diagnostic results; these are reporting bands, not permission to deploy or a claim that 20% is guaranteed. Prefer <=20% drawdown for deployment consideration; explicitly flag higher-risk candidates and insolvency, margin or liquidation events. No candidate can win final selection on profit alone if execution or independent validation fails.
- Explore Canadian and US equities (TSX, TSXV, CSE, NYSE, NASDAQ, NYSE American), ETFs, actual options and multi-leg spreads, futures including micro contracts, crypto, metals, commodities, FX and bonds where lawful and realistically accessible to a Canadian resident. Support intraday, swing, position, long/short and complementary multi-strategy portfolios as data and execution models permit. No proxy pricing passed off as actual option/future trades. Do not claim research coverage for unsupported markets or assume every instrument is available through Wealthsimple. Keep account/broker feasibility and tax treatment explicit; no live execution.
- Do not require all asset classes to be ready to start the first frozen eligible scope. Keep unsupported markets in a separate backlog with coverage gaps and separate later manifests; do not silently change the frozen campaign universe or quotas.

### Comparison and selection
- Compare candidates and VMM using identical capital, dates, costs, and eligible instrument universe wherever a matched comparator is possible. Target >=3x VMM NET PROFIT when VMM net profit is positive; calculate absolute excess profit as well. When VMM is zero or negative, the ratio is undefined/not meaningful: use absolute profit, risk, cash and appropriate passive baseline; do not label a 3x winner. For instruments without a valid matched VMM, use an appropriate pre-registered asset benchmark and report separately, not a false 3x claim.
- Require chronological development and rolling/walk-forward tests, independently sealed holdout, embargo where relevant, multiple-testing accounting (including correlated trials), parameter perturbations, cost stress and block-bootstrap uncertainty before final promotion. No final holdout queries during bulk search or live leaderboard generation. Keep exact trade/equity replays for finalists. A development threshold crossing is a research lead, not a validated winner.
- Risk must use marked-to-market account equity, including open positions and realistic gap/margin losses; closed-trade drawdown is screening-only. Respect finite shared capital, liquidity, position sizing, instrument multipliers, actual spreads, execution, FX, borrow, fees, funding and corporate actions. No unlimited leverage, reused capital across simultaneous holdings or hypothetical fills. Report risk of ruin estimates with assumptions, not guarantees.
- Freeze selection rules and final shortlist before accessing holdout; do not reselect on the holdout. Rank candidates by development net profit within risk bands and preserve diverse strategy families, not only near-duplicates of one lucky signal. Maintain top candidates per market/timeframe/style plus cross-market portfolio research. Compare independent evidence against VMM and passive/cash benchmarks. GPT and Claude review after the local campaign; paper trade before considering any real capital.
- No automatic deployment, no additional purchase, no live VMM modifications.
