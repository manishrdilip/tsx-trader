## GPT–Claude validation survival funnel — approved implementation direction

Implement an auditable, local validation pipeline after candidate discovery. The initial billion evaluations identify leads, not validated winners. Preserve maximum executable compounded net profit per comparable time horizon as the primary objective, lower capital loss and drawdown as competing risk objectives, and zero win-rate optimization weight. Allow zero survivors.

### Sequential gates
1. **Eligibility and replay:** verify immutable identifiers, point-in-time universe, data provenance, causal features, realistic fills and costs, corporate actions, shared account capital and asset-specific execution. Reject impossible trades, insolvency and unsupported instruments. Reproduce finalist trade and mark-to-market equity paths.
2. **Selection-bias control:** pre-register Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) and/or an appropriate bootstrap maximum-statistic or other multiplicity-aware method before production screening. Account for skew/kurtosis, serial dependence, correlated trials, search across datasets and all selection/adaptation stages. Distinguish actual trial count from effective independent trials; document estimation and uncertainty, use conservative sensitivity bounds, and preserve enough return-series or deterministic replay evidence from the full search and representative rejected candidates. Do not apply a nominal single-strategy p-value to the selected winner, and do not claim a correction guarantees a real edge. DSR uses a Sharpe-based statistic and does not by itself establish significance of net-profit selection; align additional tests with the actual selection procedure. If evidence is insufficient, mark inconclusive.
3. **Development walk-forward:** use chronological rolling/expanding train and forward evaluation folds, purging/embargo as needed. Dates 2022–2024 are proposed development folds subject to data coverage, not a claim of untouched data. If these years influenced selection, report their results as development evidence; use nested selection or fresh forward windows for unbiased estimates. Preserve fold-level net profit, CAGR, loss, drawdown, exposure, turnover, trade count, and VMM/passive comparison.
4. **Parameter robustness:** pre-register nearby parameter perturbations, test realistic adjacent window/threshold/exit settings and inspect return/risk plateaus. Treat neighbors used for tuning as additional development trials. Do not require all genuinely discontinuous mechanisms to have smooth plateaus; document exceptions and uncertainty.
5. **Execution stress:** baseline and adverse spread/slippage/fees, including a doubled-cost scenario where meaningful, liquidity and gaps, funding/borrow/FX/margin where applicable. Use adverse fill assumptions and compare profitability, marked-to-market risk and recovery. Double realistic modeled costs rather than pretending doubling an arbitrary flat cost is complete stress testing.
6. **Transfer and concentration:** test related eligible instruments and market regimes, held-out symbols where possible, and portfolio capital constraints. Assess whether profit depends on a single ticker, one period, a few extreme trades or regime. Asset-specific strategies need not succeed in unrelated markets; define the intended domain beforehand. Evaluate returns with uncertainty, dependent trades and effective sample size. Around 100 trades is a diagnostic guideline, not a mandatory gate; sparse strategies need longer history or stronger corroboration. No minimum win rate.
7. **Frozen shortlist:** retain rolling top-100 per approved scope/metric/risk category and diverse families. After development gates, target 5–20 finalists (not a quota that forces survivors); freeze strategy code, parameter hashes, allocation, costs, benchmark, dates, and selection/acceptance criteria before unlocking any final holdout. GPT and Claude independently review the same development evidence and protocol. Do not use the holdout for subsequent selection or tuning.
8. **One-time independent validation:** verify the actual history of prior research before designating a holdout. 2025+ is a proposed date range only, not proven independent given earlier project experiments. Quarantine previously accessed dates/symbols as appropriate; if no defensible independent holdout remains, use prospectively collected data or genuinely untouched licensed datasets and report provisional status. Evaluate frozen finalists once using a pre-registered multiplicity-aware protocol. Compare matched VMM net profit on identical capital/dates/costs when meaningful, with separate CAD/USD tracks; report appropriate benchmark otherwise. Report net profit per month/year, CAGR with uncertainty, maximum drawdown, tail loss, stability, capital utilization, time to double/recover, and trade distribution. No forced 3x winner.
9. **Forward paper trading:** run the frozen strategy without real money for a pre-registered duration and enough opportunities for its frequency, commonly several months or longer. Compare observed execution, returns and risk to expected ranges. No live deployment is authorized.
